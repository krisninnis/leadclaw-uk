# LeadClaw CRM Pipeline Workflow

## Goal

Move leads from discovery to customer using a clear, measurable pipeline.

---

# Pipeline Stages

## 1. New Lead

Lead imported from:

- Lead Finder
- Website Audit
- Contact Form
- Referral
- Manual Entry

Actions:

- Generate AI Audit
- Generate Outreach Draft

---

## 2. Qualified

Lead meets target criteria.

Checks:

- Industry fit
- Website quality
- Contact details available
- Geographic fit

Actions:

- Assign priority
- Queue for outreach

---

## 3. Outreach Ready

AI-generated outreach available.

Actions:

- Review email
- Edit message
- Approve

---

## 4. Contacted

Initial outreach sent.

Track:

- Sent date
- Channel
- Message version

Actions:

- Schedule follow-up

---

## 5. Engaged

Lead has:

- Replied
- Booked demo
- Requested information

Actions:

- Create tasks
- Add notes
- Track objections

---

## 6. Demo Scheduled

Track:

- Date
- Attendees
- Outcome

Actions:

- Prepare proposal
- Send reminders

---

## 7. Proposal Sent

Track:

- Proposal value
- Date sent
- Expected close date

---

## 8. Won

Customer converted.

Actions:

- Start onboarding
- Provision workspace
- Activate subscription

---

## 9. Lost

Lead not progressing.

Reasons:

- Budget
- Competitor
- No response
- Timing
- Not a fit

Store reason for reporting.

---

# Lead Scores

## Hot

80–100

## Warm

50–79

## Cold

0–49

---

# KPIs

Track:

- Leads imported
- Qualified leads
- Outreach sent
