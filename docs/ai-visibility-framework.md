# LeadClaw AI Visibility Framework

## Purpose

The AI Visibility Score measures how likely a business is to be discovered, referenced, and recommended by AI systems such as:

- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- Claude
- Perplexity
- Copilot
- Google AI Overviews

---

# AI Visibility Score

Maximum Score: 100

---

# Category 1: Entity Authority (20)

Measures whether the business is clearly identifiable as a trusted entity.

## Checks

- Consistent business name
- Consistent contact details
- About page
- Team page
- Company information
- Structured business information

### Scoring

0–5 Poor

6–10 Average

11–15 Good

16–20 Excellent

---

# Category 2: Topical Authority (20)

Measures whether the website demonstrates expertise.

## Checks

- Industry-specific pages
- Educational content
- FAQ coverage
- Service depth
- Industry resources

### Scoring

0–5 Poor

6–10 Average

11–15 Good

16–20 Excellent

---

# Category 3: AI Citation Readiness (20)

Measures whether AI systems can easily quote and summarise content.

## Checks

- Clear headings
- Structured content
- Lists
- FAQs
- Definitions
- Comparisons

### Scoring

0–5 Poor

6–10 Average

11–15 Good

16–20 Excellent

# Category 4: Comparison Content (20)

Measures whether the business appears in decision-making searches.

## Checks

- Competitor comparisons
- Alternative pages
- Versus pages
- Buyer guides

Examples:

- LeadClaw vs Answering Service
- LeadClaw vs Virtual Receptionist
- Best AI Receptionist UK

### Scoring

0–5 Poor

6–10 Average

11–15 Good

16–20 Excellent

---

# Category 5: Original Authority Signals (20)

Measures unique information that AI systems value.

## Checks

- Industry statistics
- Research
- Case studies
- Surveys
- Proprietary data
- Original insights

### Scoring

0–5 Poor

6–10 Average

11–15 Good

16–20 Excellent

---

# Final AI Visibility Grades

90–100 = Dominant

80–89 = Strong

70–79 = Competitive

60–69 = Emerging

Below 60 = Invisible
