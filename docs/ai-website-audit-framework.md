# LeadClaw AI Website Audit Framework

## Overview

The AI Website Audit scores a business website out of 100 and identifies the fastest improvements that will increase visibility, lead generation, trust, and AI discoverability.

---

# Score Categories

| Category            | Weight |
| ------------------- | ------ |
| Technical SEO       | 20     |
| Local SEO           | 15     |
| Conversion          | 20     |
| Trust & Credibility | 15     |
| Content Quality     | 15     |
| AI Visibility       | 15     |

Total = 100

---

# Technical SEO (20)

## Checks

- Sitemap exists
- robots.txt exists
- Mobile responsive
- HTTPS enabled
- Page titles present
- Meta descriptions present
- H1 tags present
- Internal linking
- Page speed acceptable

Scoring:

0-5 Poor

6-10 Weak

11-15 Good

16-20 Excellent

---

# Local SEO (15)

## Checks

- Address displayed
- Phone number displayed
- Service area pages
- Google Business Profile link
- Location pages
- Local schema markup

Scoring:

0-5 Poor

6-10 Good

11-15 Excellent

---

# Conversion (20)

## Checks

- Clear headline
- Clear call to action
- Contact form
- Phone number
- Lead magnet
- Booking/demo option
- Multiple CTAs

Scoring:

0-5 Poor

6-10 Average

11-15 Good

16-20 Excellent

# Final Grades

90-100 = Elite

80-89 = Strong

70-79 = Good

60-69 = Needs Improvement

Below 60 = At Risk
